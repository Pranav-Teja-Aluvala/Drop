Cookies & local data

What lives in your browser, and for how long. Updated 25 October 2026.

CookiesYour roomHostingYour controls

No app tracking cookies

Drop does not set application cookies for pairing, install analytics or advertising SDKs, or read cookies from your other websites. There are no optional tracking controls in this build, so there is no decorative consent banner.

A service worker saves only the static offline error page, its interaction script and favicon in Cache Storage after an online visit. It does not cache room pages, invitation URLs, files or messages. This lets Drop show a useful error if a later navigation has no connection. You can remove it through your browser’s site-data controls. Optional tracking would need separate disclosure and controls before activation.

Your room is temporary

Room credentials, connection state, file buffers and chat messages are held in the open page’s memory. Drop does not persist conversations in localStorage, sessionStorage or IndexedDB. The interface shows up to 40 file activity entries and the latest 200 messages.

Starting a new room or closing the page clears its unsaved conversation and releases file references. Ending a room stops transfers; completed items remain available on the open page. Saved downloads remain on your device until you delete them. Browser-managed memory is not a promise of forensic erasure.

Hosting is separate

Vercel hosts the website, Render runs pairing and Cloudflare provides STUN. Providers receive connection or request metadata and may apply their own security technologies. Drop cannot use room expiry to delete provider-controlled logs. The privacy policy links their policies and explains their roles.

You stay in control

Files require acceptance and an explicit save action. Nothing is automatically opened. Copying a message requires your action; Drop does not monitor your system clipboard.

For privacy questions, visit the Contact page. Do not include private files, invitations or security codes.
