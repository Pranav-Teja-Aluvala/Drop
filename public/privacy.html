What you send

Drop sends files and notes between two paired browsers using a WebRTC data channel, encrypted in transit. The recipient chooses whether to accept a file. File integrity is checked before a download is offered. Files are not scanned for malware.

Pairing and room information

The pairing server holds random room identifiers, temporary credentials, connection messages and expiry information in memory. It receives network addresses. Rooms last up to two hours and end when a paired device disconnects or you end the room. Invites expire earlier. Approve only the device you intended and compare security codes on both screens.

What remains on your device

This build has no persistent file database or recipient signup. Received files and notes remain in the page’s memory until the room is reset or the page is closed. Downloads you save remain on your device. Clipboard content is not read automatically; copying requires your action.

The application does not install analytics or advertising trackers and does not set application cookies for pairing. Hosting infrastructure may process request metadata independently.

Services involved in a connection

Cloudflare’s public STUN service helps browsers establish a connection and receives network metadata, not file contents. If a TURN relay is configured, encrypted traffic may pass through that provider. Hosting and relay operators may process IP addresses and operational logs. Their production configuration and retention must be confirmed before public launch.

Your choices and questions

You can decline incoming files, end your room, close the page and remove any downloaded files through your device. Keep invite links private. Drop does not provide anonymous networking or a malware guarantee.

The operator’s identity and privacy contact will be published before public launch.
