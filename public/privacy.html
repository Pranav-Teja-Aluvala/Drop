Your files. Your control.

Privacy policy · Updated 7 November 2026

What shareEm handles

shareEm connects up to three browsers in a temporary room. Files and notes use encrypted WebRTC data channels between approved devices. You choose the recipient; files need acceptance before download. All extensions are allowed within the 50 MB limit. Files are not scanned for malware or opened automatically.

Rooms and device approval

The pairing service holds room identifiers, a six-letter recognition name, private invitation and per-device credentials, approval state, connection messages and expiry times in memory. It sees network addresses. The room name alone cannot grant access. The owner approves each new device once.

shareEm trusts the website and pairing service to introduce devices. V2 does not provide an independent certificate-code comparison. Anyone with the private invite can request admission, so allow only a device you intended.

Rooms last up to two hours. An approved device can return within ten minutes after backgrounding or losing its socket, using a credential kept in that open page. Visibility and disconnection deadlines are sent to the server. Expired devices are removed; if the owner expires or ends the room, the whole room closes. A server restart loses room state.

What stays in your browser

Files and messages stay in page memory. Tab session storage holds a temporary return credential and the owner’s invite so the same tab can rejoin after a reload. Chat is limited to the latest 200 messages and file activity to 40 entries. Saved downloads remain on your device. Reloading, closing or browser termination can lose the page’s credentials and unsaved data. Interrupted files are not automatically resumed.

Only static error artwork, its script and favicon are cached for offline navigation. No room URLs, files, credentials or messages enter that cache. There are no application tracking cookies or analytics SDKs. See Cookies & local data.

Network services

Vercel hosts the website, Render runs the pairing service and Cloudflare supplies STUN. Providers can receive IP addresses and operational request metadata under their own policies. Room expiry does not delete provider logs.

No TURN relay is configured. Some networks cannot connect directly. Peers may learn each other’s network addresses. STUN does not carry file contents.

Your choices

Decline files, leave or end a room, close the page, or clear browser site data. Saved downloads must be removed through your file manager. Clipboard copying and native invitation sharing happen only after your action. If you use Share invite, the OS and selected destination receive that link.

Maintainer details and the address for support or privacy requests are on Contact us. Support email is processed by Gmail to answer your request. There is no fixed support-email retention period yet; you may request deletion subject to applicable retention duties. Do not send private files or room credentials.
