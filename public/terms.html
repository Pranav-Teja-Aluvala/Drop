Using Drop

Use Drop for files and information you are authorized to share. Allow only your intended devices. Do not use it for unlawful content, abuse, or attempts to access another person’s room. The private invitation is sensitive; the six-letter name is only a recognition aid.

Limits and safe sharing

Rooms support up to three devices and last up to two hours. Initial invitations expire after ten minutes. A five-digit code can request access during the invitation window. Each new device needs the owner’s approval once. Automatic file acceptance is optional, applies to approved devices in the current room, and resets when you leave or reload. Choose a recipient before sending; only that device receives the file or message.

Files are limited to 50 MiB (labelled 50 MB in the interface). One file per direction per connection can be active at a time. Notes are limited to 50,000 characters. All file types are accepted; files and archives are not scanned for malware. Keep your originals.

Leaving and returning

Approved devices have a return window of up to ten minutes when the page is hidden, locked or disconnected. Returning in the same tab can restore connections without another approval. An expired owner ends the room. Browser termination, reloads, network restrictions and server restarts can prevent recovery. Interrupted transfers must be sent again.

Availability

This build is free and has no purchases, subscription or checkout; a purchase refund process does not apply. No paid uptime guarantee is offered. No TURN relay is enabled, and some networks cannot connect. The free pairing host may sleep or restart. Do not rely on this service as your only copy of a file.
