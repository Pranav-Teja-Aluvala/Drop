Using Drop

Use Drop only for files and information you are authorized to share. Do not use it to distribute unlawful content, attempt unauthorized access, interfere with other rooms or overload the service. You keep your rights to your files; transferring them does not transfer ownership to Drop.

Share with the intended device

Keep room invitations private. Approve only the device you intended, compare every security-code group and accept only files you expect. Drop blocks common executable formats but does not scan for malware. You decide whether to save received files.

Rooms and limits

This build has no signup or checkout. Rooms last up to two hours. Initial invitations expire after ten minutes and are consumed on approval. The implementation supports one file per direction at a time, up to 50 MiB (labelled 50 MB in the interface), and notes up to 50,000 characters.

Keep both pages open until delivery is confirmed. Backgrounding a phone or closing a tab can interrupt a transfer. Keep your own original files and backups.

Availability and privacy

The service is in development. Network conditions and relay availability affect whether devices can connect. There is no published uptime commitment or paid service in this build. See the privacy policy for data handling.
