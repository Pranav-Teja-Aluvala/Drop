Before you write

Include your browser, device and a short description of what happened. If a message appeared on screen, quote it without personal details.

Do not send private files, room invitations, security codes or passwords. There is no need to attach a file just to ask about a transfer.

Some networks cannot connect in this direct-only beta. If pairing fails, try both devices on the same Wi-Fi with VPNs off. Keep both pages open while sending.

Privacy questions and deletion requests

Use the same support address for privacy questions or a request to delete support correspondence. Drop cannot retrieve a file or conversation from a closed room. Saved downloads remain on your device; you remove them through your device’s file manager.

See Privacy and Cookies & local data for what the application and its providers handle. There is no published support response-time guarantee.
